Brand identity · Round 1

Doogan & Co

Four logo directions for a professional consultancy serving civil construction and plant hire. Each one is shown on white, on navy, and at the small sizes it will need for email signatures, favicons and social profiles.

Concept A

The Wordmark

The name set in EB Garamond, with an italic ampersand in blue as the single point of colour. It reads as established and trustworthy, like a law or accounting firm, and works anywhere text works.

Concept B

The Benchmark

The symbol is the surveyor's benchmark: a broad arrow pointing up to a level line, cut into kerbs and walls to mark a fixed height everyone builds from. It says "a reliable reference point" in the language of the industry Doogan & Co works in.

Concept C

The Stacked Lockup

A heavy, widely spaced surname over a blue rule, finished with a soft italic "& Co." The contrast between the two faces gives it both strength and warmth. It suits a document cover, a hard-hat sticker or a sign.

Concept D

The Monogram

A "D" with a blue ampersand tucked against it, inside a navy tile. This is the profile picture and favicon version. It pairs with Concept A, which shares the same typeface.

How to choose

  1. Pick the concept that feels most like the business. You can also combine two, for example Concept A as the main logo with Concept D as the icon.
  2. Tell Claude what to change: colour, weight, spacing, or a tagline under the name.
  3. Once you are happy, ask for final files. Claude will export SVG and PNG versions with the lettering converted to shapes, so they look the same on any computer.
